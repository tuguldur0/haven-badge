PARTS (read the drawings in parts\ and verify every number)
- U1: RP2040-Zero clone, pins pre-soldered, plugs into female 2.54 mm headers (1x9 x2 for the side rows; add the bottom row only if a needed GPIO is there). Use parts\module for the pinout, dimensions and schematic. Waveshare says the 5V pin is tied directly to USB VBUS. GP16 drives the module's own WS2812 LED; leave it alone.
- DS1: 2.0" ST7789 240x320 SPI module, Goldenmorning GMT020-02-8P, 8 pins. Front view, pins at top, left to right: 1 GND (square pad), 2 VCC, 3 SCL, 4 SDA, 5 RST, 6 DC, 7 CS, 8 BL. Plugs into a 1x8 female socket and sits on standoffs at its 4 corner holes. Take the outline, hole spacing and pin-row position from the drawing in parts\display. If there is no dimension drawing, tell me and stop. The back has what looks like a regulator (Q1, "3-5 V" module) and a backlight transistor (Q2 near BL); confirm from the photo.
- Power: 3xAA holder with its own switch and a JST-PH 2.0 lead, mounted on the back with foam tape (reserve the area; I'll give holder dimensions later). J5 is a JST-PH 2-pin through-hole vertical connector. D1 SS34 from BAT+ to the module's 5V pin, so USB can't back-feed the cells. 10 uF bulk on 5V.
- Display VCC: default to the 5V rail after D1, with a solder jumper to choose 3V3 instead. Tell me which you think is right after reading the module schematic and the display photo.
- Backlight: BL to a PWM-capable GPIO through 100 ohm.
- Battery sense: BAT+ (before D1) through a 100k/100k divider with 100 nF to an ADC pin (GP26-29).
- Buttons: 8x 6x6 mm through-hole tact switches (UP, DOWN, LEFT, RIGHT, A, B, START, SELECT) to GND, using internal pull-ups.
- Hackability: an unpopulated 2.54 mm expansion header footprint with GND, 3V3 and the spare GPIOs, ideally an I2C pair.
- No other parts. No vibration motor, no buzzer.
